Project Understanding

The website should now reflect the company’s next phase.

Our Process

How the work will be planned, built, reviewed, and launched.

This section explains the work behind the quotation. The scope is not for only page design; it covers business understanding, website structure, content planning, compliance-safe presentation, responsive development, review cycles, and launch support.

What makes this different from a basic brochure website?

Zodiac JRD MKJ Ltd. needs a corporate website that explains a business transition clearly: diamond legacy in the background and automotive components as the main direction. The website also needs Investor Relations structure, business inquiry flows, responsive layouts, and content that can be approved safely for a listed company.

Automotive-first communication The website must make tooling, injection moulded parts, manufacturing capability, and B2B credibility easy to understand.
Investor-safe structure Investor Relations sections, disclosures, document uploads, and public information need to be organised clearly.
Design direction selection We will share selected design references, layout formats, and a moodboard so the company can choose the preferred direction before full design begins.
Approval-friendly workflow Copy, visuals, and product/factory details will be structured so the company can review and approve before publishing.
Step 01

Discovery & business mapping

We first finalise the exact website objective, target stakeholders, automotive vertical details, diamond legacy positioning, Investor Relations needs, and content approval process.

Step 02

Sitemap and page structure

We prepare the website structure around Home, About, Automotive Components, Tooling, Injection Moulded Parts, Diamond Legacy, Investor Relations, Helpdesk, and Contact.

Step 03

Content planning and copy support

We convert company inputs into clear website copy. Product claims, facility details, customer names, capacity, and forward-looking statements will be used only after approval.

Step 04

Design references & moodboard

Before designing the full website, we will share selected design references, possible layout formats, colour direction, typography direction, and a moodboard so the company can clearly see the proposed visual direction.

Step 05

Direction selection & approval

The company will review and select the preferred design direction. Once the direction is approved, we will lock the overall style, page format, content hierarchy, and visual treatment before moving into detailed UI design.

Step 06

Responsive UI design

We design the website for desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile using the approved direction. The layout will stay clean, corporate, and easy to navigate without overusing animation or decorative effects.

Step 07

Website development

After the design direction and key layouts are approved, we build the website pages, navigation, inquiry forms, IR sections, analytics setup, basic SEO/indexing setup, and responsive behaviour.

Step 08

Review, testing and launch

We test responsiveness, links, forms, content placement, page consistency, and basic performance before launch. Domain and hosting setup assistance is included; actual cost is paid by the client.
